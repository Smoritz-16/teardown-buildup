01 · Engine

V10 · Huracán 5.2

A 90° V10 with glass liners, so you watch the charge burn. Ten rods share five crankpins 72° apart, so the firing order 1-6-5-10-2-7-3-8-4-9 gives power strokes 90°, then 54°, apart. That uneven rhythm is where the howl comes from.

02 · Watch

Daytona · Calibre 4131

A self-winding chronograph seen from the case back. The balance swings about ±300° four times a second, the escape wheel moves half a tooth each beat, and the column wheel and vertical clutch start the stopwatch. Drag the crown to wind the mainspring.

How it works

One input drives everything
01One number

The engine runs from crank angle and the watch from the movement's own time. Every part's position and every readout is a function of that one value, so nothing drifts.

02Real dimensions

Bore, stroke, rod length, firing order, beat rate and power reserve come from published specs. The pistons follow the exact slider-crank equation.

03Open it up

The part doing the work opens up: cut the liners, x-ray the bridges, explode the layers, isolate one cylinder. Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom.

Study models built in three.js with the open-source teardown kit (MIT). Not affiliated with Lamborghini, Audi or Rolex. Watch tooth counts are illustrative; the ratios are real.
